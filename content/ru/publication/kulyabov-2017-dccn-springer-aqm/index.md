abstract: The article is devoted to the application of Markov modu- lated arrival
  processes (Markov modulated Poisson process — MMPP, Markov modulated Bernoulli process
  — MMBP and Markov modulated fluid flow — MMFF) models to the analysis of Active
  Queue Manage- ment (AQM) algorithms (Random Early Detection (RED) family, for example).
  The main ideas and properties of Markov modulated arrival processes (MMAP) are presented
  as the brief description of RED-type AQM algorithms. A review of the main results
  obtained with the help of MMAP processes in the analysis of AQM algorithms models
  is made. The authors formulated problems that also can be solved with the help of
  MMAP processes when analysing the systems with RED-like algorithms.
